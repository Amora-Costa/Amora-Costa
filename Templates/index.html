Welcome to AMORA COSTA Education and Consultant

The gate way for your global opportunities

At AmORA COSTA, we believe that every individual deserves the chance to grow, succeed, and secure a better future — no matter their background or skill level. As a trusted international job consultancy, we specialize in connecting both skilled professionals and unskilled workers with genuine and rewarding job opportunities abroad. Whether you're a technician, nurse, IT expert, hotel staff, factory worker, driver, or laborer, our expert team is here to guide you through every step — from job matching and visa processing to travel arrangements and post-placement support.

WHAT WE OFFER

  • Global Job Placement for Skilled & Unskilled Roles

    • Global job placement involves recruiting and deploying workers from one country to another to meet workforce demands in industries like construction, healthcare, agriculture, hospitality, manufacturing, etc.

  • Verified Employers Across Middle East, Europe, and Asia

    • Verified employers are companies or agencies that have passed a background screening process to ensure they are legitimate, reliable, and compliant with labor laws. These employers are officially approved to recruit foreign workers.

  • Personalized Career Guidance

    • helps individuals make informed decisions about their career path, based on their skills, interests, qualifications, work experience, and market demand.

  • Hassle-Free Visa & Documentation Support

    • streamlined service that assists job seekers in preparing, submitting, and managing all paperwork required for international job placements. It's especially crucial for those applying abroad in the Middle East, Europe, or Asia, where immigration rules can be complex.

  • Affordable & Transparent Services

    • ensure that job seekers and employers clearly understand the costs, benefits, and processes involved in global job placement—without hidden fees, misleading promises, or last-minute surprises.
